LAB 2 · Commit before you analyse

Four defensible answers. One is the constraint.

Systems · Constraints · Agents

A

Email response time

Bids arrive too close to the deadline. The RFQ cycle is simply too short.

B

Administrative burden

Clarification rounds have climbed. The team is drowning in back-and-forth.

C

Vendor response rate

Fewer of the vendors we invite bother to reply at all.

D

The healthy vendor pool

There are no longer enough vendors in good standing to run a competitive RFQ.

Every pod writes a letter on a card and turns it face down. We open them together after the analysis.

All four are defensible from a first read of the data. That is deliberate — if the answer were obvious the exercise would teach nothing.

Expect the room to split mostly between B and C. Those are the loud ones.

Do not hint. The value of the reveal is proportional to how committed they were to the wrong answer.
