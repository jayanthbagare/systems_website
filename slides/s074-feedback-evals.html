III · Subsystem 5

Feedback/Evals

Systems · Constraints · Agents

How the agent knows whether what it did actually worked.

Models, like people, will confidently declare a job done. The harness must verify, not trust. Tests, validators, downstream signals — anything that closes the loop with reality.

Of the five subsystems, this is the highest-leverage one. The shortest path to a reliable agent is almost always through better, faster, more honest feedback.

Quote from the harness engineering literature: when you remove components one at a time and measure the drop, feedback is usually the one whose removal hurts most. This is the lever to teach the room.
