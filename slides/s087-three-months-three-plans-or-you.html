LAB 5 · The test

Three months. Three plans. Or you built a sorting function.

Systems · Constraints · Agents

Month 5

Pool healthy. Minor copper movement.

The plan should look like the standard playbook — eight vendors, seven-day window. Sameness here is correct.

Month 10

Guangzhou collapsing, cash stress at its peak.

The plan should shrink the invite list, extend the bid window, and say out loud that the pool may fall short.

Month 15

Rare earth shock. A competitor bidding for the same vendor capacity.

The plan should diversify across categories and flag the capacity contention explicitly.

The adaptation note is the deliverable. ‘The pool is smaller than usual’ is not an adaptation note.

A real one names what it changed, by how much, and which signal caused the change.

Month 5 is included precisely so that not adapting is sometimes the right answer. An agent that dramatises a calm month is as broken as one that ignores a crisis.

Read two pods' month-10 adaptation notes aloud, unattributed. The difference between vague and specific will be obvious to the room without you naming it.
