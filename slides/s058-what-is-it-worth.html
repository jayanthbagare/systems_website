Watch for the trap: revenue went up 36%. If your model concludes that things improved, you are doing cost accounting with different labels.

EM owns the model · PM owns the CFO sentence · devs resist the urge to build a dashboard

The trap is real and most pods fall into it. Revenue genuinely grew. A cost-accounting reflex reads that as success.

If a pod is stuck, give them one nudge: compute T as a percentage of revenue, month by month, and look at the shape.

Do not give them the breakeven number. The defensible-out-loud part matters more than the figure.
