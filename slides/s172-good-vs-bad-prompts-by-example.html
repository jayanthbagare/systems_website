Good vs Bad Prompts — By Example

PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore | LLM & AI Foundations

✗ Vague Prompt

"Tell me about holidays

in Tamil Nadu"

•No year specified

•No format requested

•No role or context

•Could return essays, lists, or anything

✓ Specific Prompt

"List all gazetted holidays in

Tamil Nadu for 2026 as a table

with date, day, holiday name,

and whether it's optional."

•Year and state specified

•Format clearly requested (table)

•Specific fields listed

•Scope narrowed (gazetted only)

Prompting Principles

•Be specific — vague in, vague out

•Provide context — who are you? what's this for?

•Use examples (few-shot) — show, don't just tell

•Specify format — JSON, table, bullet points, code

•Iterate — your first prompt is rarely your best

•Use chain-of-thought — ask the model to "think step by step"
