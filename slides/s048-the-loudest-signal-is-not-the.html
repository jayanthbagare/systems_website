II · The discipline

The loudest signal is not the constraint

Systems · Constraints · Agents

B — Administrative burden

Clarification rounds rise after the health scores fall. It is downstream.

Eliminate it entirely and throughput does not move — there still are not enough vendors to bid.

C — Vendor response rate

The mechanism, not the constraint.

Ask 'how do we get more replies?' and you buy chasing tools. Ask 'why are healthy vendors deprioritising us?' and you find the late payments.

A — Cycle time

Seven to ten days is a policy parameter, not a system output.

Nothing is being constrained here. It is a number someone chose.

A symptom moves when you push it. A constraint moves the whole system when you push it.

Test for any candidate: if I fixed this completely and changed nothing else, would throughput rise?

Give real credit to pods that answered B or C. Both are honest readings and both are what most organisations act on.

The counterfactual test at the bottom is the transferable tool. Make them say it back to you in their own words before moving on.
