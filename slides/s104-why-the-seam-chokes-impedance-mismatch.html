THE SEAM

Why the Seam Chokes: Impedance Mismatch

Two subsystems, two natures. The handoff is lossy wherever they differ — and rate is only the first way they differ.

RATE

Fast system feeds a slow one. Queues, buffers, starvation.

You know this one — it's the TOC bottleneck.

FORM

Structured data meets unstructured judgment. A clean record arrives at a human inbox.

The schema ends where the person begins.

MEANING

Same word, different object. Finance's "vendor" is not procurement's "vendor".

The handoff silently drops meaning.

TRUST

One side won't accept what the other sends. Re-checks, re-approvals, shadow spreadsheets.

The choke is disbelief, not throughput.

Rate you already know how to fix. The other three are why the seam is still choked.

Start at rate BECAUSE it bridges from the TOC first half — they know the buffer-or-starve problem. Then widen: rate is the cleanest mismatch, not the deepest.

Form, meaning, trust — these are the mismatches that actually choke enterprises, and they're the new material. Ontology and epistemology (coming) aren't answers to a rate problem — they're answers to meaning and trust problems. This slide sets up everything downstream.
