LAB 6 · 45 minutes · Your pod

Your delivery cycle is a system too

Systems · Constraints · Agents

Same lens, turned around. Not the customer's process — yours.

What you do

Draw your two-to-four week cycle as stations.

Where does work actually pile up?

Which station is always behind while the others wait politely?

What you produce

One named constraint in your own cycle.

One exploit needing no headcount and no budget.

An honest altitude call: automation, agent, or neither.

How you know you're done

You can say whether an agent belongs there — and most pods will honestly say not yet.

If your constraint turns out to be code review, or a shared test environment, or the one person who understands the integration — that is a real finding. Say it out loud. It is worth more than a plausible agent.

Whole pod, no split roles · EM writes it down · PM presents to one other pod

This is the lab that makes the two days pay for themselves. Everything before it was rehearsal on someone else's data.

Expect discomfort. Naming your own constraint in front of your own EM is harder than naming Meridian's.

Pair pods for the share-out rather than presenting to the room — it is more honest and it fits the clock.

If a pod concludes 'no agent belongs here', celebrate it explicitly. That is the discipline the whole workshop is for.
