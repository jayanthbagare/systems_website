Prompt Patterns That Work

PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore | LLM & AI Foundations

Zero-Shot

•Just ask directly — no examples

•"Classify this email as spam or not"

•Works well for simple tasks

•Relies entirely on model's training

Few-Shot

•Provide 2–5 examples first

•"Email: 'Buy now!' → Spam"

•"Email: 'Meeting at 3' → Not spam"

•Now classify this new email…

Chain-of-Thought

•"Think step by step"

•"Show your reasoning before answering"

•Dramatically improves accuracy on reasoning tasks

•Essential for math, logic, multi-step problems

Role Prompting

•"You are a senior database admin…"

•Sets expertise level & perspective

•"Act as a Tamil Nadu govt official who manages leave policies"

•Shapes vocabulary, depth, and style

Structured Output

•"Return as JSON / XML / CSV"

•"Use this exact schema: {...}"

•Critical for programmatic use

•Most models follow JSON reliably

Iterative Refinement

•"Good, now also include weekends"

•"Make it more concise"

•"Add error handling"

•Treat the LLM as a collaborator
