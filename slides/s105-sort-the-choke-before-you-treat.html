THE SEAM

Sort the Choke Before You Treat It

Not all chokes are the same kind of thing — and the type dictates the tool.

MECHANICAL

Physics of the system. Time delay, batch windows, capacity gaps.

Buffer it, or re-engineer it.

Pure drag. Remove without regret.

HUMAN

Someone's interest is served by the choke. Politics, turf, fear of blame.

Realign incentives — but look first.

Sometimes the politics is protecting something real. Drag or signal — check which.

SEMANTIC

The two sides don't mean the same thing — or don't trust the same knowledge.

This one needs a map.

The only choke that ontology and epistemology can treat. Next two slides.

Some chokes are drag. Some are signal. Sorting them is the discipline.

The typology is what earns the right to introduce ontology at all. Without the sort, ontology arrives as a non-sequitur.

Mechanical → buffer or re-engineer. Pure drag.

Human → realign incentives, BUT: sometimes the politics is protecting something real. Drag or signal — check first. (Same cut as the override logs: the human choke can be a sensor.)

Semantic → the only one that needs a map. That's the next two slides.

Don't rush the third column — it's the doorway.
