II · A different lens

Throughput accounting — three quantities

Systems · Constraints · Agents

T

Throughput

The rate at which the system generates value through sales — not output, not utilisation.

OE

Operating Expense

What it costs to turn potential into throughput — wages, energy, all of it.

I

Investment

Money tied up inside the system — inventory, working capital, deferred work.

Keep this light. The point is not to make accountants of them — it is to break the cost-accounting reflex.

The decision criterion under throughput accounting: an investment is worth it if Δ(T - OE) is positive and grows faster than ΔI. Crucially, time is in the numerator. Cost views are static.
