What a good loop map shows

Systems · Constraints · Agents

A loop, not a chain

If your diagram runs left to right with nothing returning, you drew a pipeline. Find the arrow that comes back.

Delays marked

Every delay is a place the system will oscillate. The unmarked ones are the ones that surprise you later.

A purpose you inferred

The purpose the behaviour implies — not the one on the org chart. They are often different.

A blind spot

What you circled as unmeasurable is the first clue for Lab 2.

Three pods share. Ninety seconds each. The PM presents.

Pick pods whose diagrams disagree with each other. Agreement this early usually means nobody looked hard enough.

Choose the three pods while walking the room during the lab, not by asking for volunteers. Pick for disagreement.

The 'something you cannot see' item is the bridge into Lab 2 — it plants the idea that the constraint may be invisible in the data they are handed.

Do not correct diagrams. Ask questions instead: what happens if this loop runs twice as fast?
