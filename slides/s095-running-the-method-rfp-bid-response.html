Where it landed

It felt frontier-worthy in imagination — and settled honestly to a hybrid. Most of the work was automation waiting to be recognised; a thin, real seam of agent discovery.

The method run once, end to end, on a fresh problem — deliberately NOT the procurement exception from Act 1.

The instructive choice: it FELT frontier-worthy in imagination and settled honestly to a hybrid — mostly automation, with a thin real seam of agent discovery (the fit analysis and narrative). That models the discipline far better than a triumphant frontier landing would.

The lesson the room takes: imagine high, but let it settle honestly. The fantasy of 'agentic RFP' resolves into 'automate the 80%, put an agent only on the genuine discovery'. That is mastery — and it's where the real, shippable value is.
