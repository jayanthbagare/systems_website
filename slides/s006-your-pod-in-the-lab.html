Your pod in the lab

Systems · Constraints · Agents

Six to eight pods, two facilitators. That only works if every role has real work.

PM

Owns the question and the share-out.

Writes the constraint statement and the sentence a CFO would repeat. Presents at every debrief.

EM

Owns the clock and the environment.

Timeboxes the lab, keeps the repo reproducible across every laptop, decides when the pod stops analysing and starts writing.

Devs

Own the data and the code.

Pair up. Two pairs beat five soloists — and the fifth person should be reading the briefing, not watching a screen.

Rule for the two days: the PM presents. If the developers present, the pod optimised locally.

This slide exists because the default failure of a mixed pod lab is that the developers take the keyboard and the PM becomes an audience.

The 'PM presents' rule is not a gimmick. It forces the pod to translate analysis into a decision someone else can act on — which is the actual job.

Walk the room in every lab looking for a PM who has gone quiet. That is the signal to intervene.
