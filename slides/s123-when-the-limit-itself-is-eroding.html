The business version

Growth funded by burning the thing that made growth possible — vendor relationships, engineer goodwill, code quality, customer trust. Each quarter's number taken out of next quarter's capacity.

A constraint you exploit recovers. A constraint you consume does not.

This is the most important slide in the systems block, because it is the shape of the case study the pods are about to analyse.

Meridian is an overshoot-and-collapse. Revenue grew 36% while the healthy vendor pool — the carrying capacity of the whole procurement system — was being consumed to pay for it. Do not say this out loud before Lab 2. Say it in the debrief and watch the room reorganise.

The closing line is a correction to a common misreading of Goldratt. 'Exploit the constraint' means get full value from it, not extract until it fails. A constraint you consume lowers the ceiling for everything downstream.
