III · Discrimination

Which bottlenecks are agent-shaped?

Systems · Constraints · Agents

Agent-shaped

•Synthesising context from many sources

•Classification under uncertainty

•Drafting that a human can edit

•Triage and prioritisation

•Repetitive judgement on a stream of cases

Not agent-shaped (yet)

•Physical capacity (more pumps, more crew)

•Hard latency (network, lab cycle time)

•Pure organisational gates (approvals)

•Regulatory sign-off requiring a named human

•Wholly novel, never-before-seen decisions

The planner's work is in the left column: synthesising context, classifying under uncertainty, drafting plans a human can edit. This is why an agent will actually move the constraint here. Be honest that not every bottleneck qualifies.
