ALTITUDE 2 · THE CRAFT

Loop Engineering

A good loop converges — reaches the goal and stops.

STOPPING CONDITION

What makes it terminate. Without one, it circles forever — or halts arbitrarily.

CHECKPOINTS

Where the human gate sits. The consequential moments where it pauses and asks.

VERIFICATION

What stops it settling on a confident wrong answer. A critic, a check, a second view.

Engineering the loop is shaping the basin — so the outcome you want is the one the loop falls into.

Kept lean on purpose — seeing outranks building.

A good loop converges: reaches the goal and STOPS. A bad one circles, or stops confidently at the wrong place — that's the ReAct failure from earlier in the deck (the confident wrong answer that persists).

Three levers: stopping condition, checkpoints, verification. One topological line, earned: engineering the loop is shaping the basin so the good outcome is the attractor the loop falls into.
