THE SEAM

No one owns this.

You've been taught to hunt the bottleneck inside the boxes. Look at the lines between them.

Inside a subsystem, someone is accountable for flow. At the boundary, no one is — which is exactly why the choke hides there, and why nobody has already fixed it.

This slide is the joint between the two halves of the whole session. The TOC first half taught them to find the constraint. This inverts the reflex: don't look inside the boxes — look at the seams.

The load-bearing line: no one owns the seam. Inside a subsystem there's accountability for flow. At the boundary there isn't. That's WHY the constraint hides there and why it survives — everyone's dashboard is green while the enterprise chokes between the dashboards.
