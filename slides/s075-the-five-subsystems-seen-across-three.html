Read it across, not down. The five subsystems are the constants. The implementations are the variables.

This table is the synthesis. The same harness model — five subsystems — admits very different concrete designs.

Hermes Agent in particular is worth understanding because of its built-in learning loop: it creates and improves its own skills from experience. That is a glimpse of where harnesses are heading — agents that don't just run skills but author them.

OpenClaw represents the layering pattern: take a vendor harness, wrap it, extend its orchestration. This is a common shape in the ecosystem and worth recognising — but it inherits its base's constraints.
