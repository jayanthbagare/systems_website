The Five Focusing Steps

Systems · Constraints · Agents

1

Identify

Find the constraint. Most teams cannot name it. That is the first failure.

2

Exploit

Squeeze every unit of throughput from the current constraint before anything else.

3

Subordinate

Make every other part of the system serve the constraint. Do not let them run ahead.

4

Elevate

Only now invest to break the constraint — add capacity, redesign, automate.

5

Repeat

The constraint will move. Go back to step 1. This never ends.

Spend the most time on Identify and Exploit. Teams almost always skip to Elevate — buying tools, hiring, adding capacity — before they have understood the constraint or squeezed what they already have. That is where money goes to die.
