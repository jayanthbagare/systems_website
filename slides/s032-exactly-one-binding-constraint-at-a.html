I · Theory of Constraints

Eliyahu Goldratt

Every non-trivial system has

exactly one binding constraint at a time.

Throughput is governed by it. Everything else is noise — or worse, distraction.

The Goal (1984) and the Theory of Constraints came out of factory floors, but the insight is general: in any flow with multiple stages, one stage governs the whole.

Systems · Constraints · Agents

Goldratt was a physicist who turned his attention to manufacturing. His core insight — that systems are governed by their constraint — feels obvious once stated, but most of us continue to act as if every part of the system contributes equally to its output. It does not.
