The RFQ-agent — what it does

Systems · Constraints · Agents

When a work order arrives, the agent runs its loop:

1

Gather context

Pulls vendor history, supplier lead times, similar prior failures and success.

2

Hypothesise

Drafts probable root cause, criticality score, required skills, schedule sensitivity.

3

Cross-check

Verifies parts availability, quality match, permit class, conflict with operations plan.

4

Compose

Sends a chosen vendor an invite for Bid, and evaluates bids

5

Classify

High confidence → into the planner's review queue. Low confidence → escalates with a question.
