I · Our example

A procurement operation — plant & supply chain

We will carry one example through the whole session. It is shaped like many of the processes

you will meet in agentic architecture and customer engagements.

Meridian Industrial Systems — a Bangalore-based manufacturer of CNC machines

and robotic assembly cells.

​

Sales orders arrive from global OEMs. Each order triggers a work order and a

procurement cycle. Hundreds of vendor bids must be solicited, tracked, evaluated,

and resolved every month.

​

Familiar? Almost every manufacturer in the world runs some version of this.

The backlog grows. The constraint is invisible. Until it is not.
