II · The reveal

One shock, seven steps, a constraint nobody named

Systems · Constraints · Agents

Sep 2024

US aluminium tariff lands.

↓

Meridian's cash position tightens — the stress index peaks in Oct–Nov.

↓

Vendors start getting paid late. Average lateness rises 40%.

↓

Vendor health scores fall. Guangzhou drops from 78 to 57.

↓

Vendors quietly deprioritise Meridian's RFQs. Alert rate climbs from 0.16 to 0.23.

↓

The pool of vendors in good standing shrinks.

↓

Fewer competitive bids per RFQ — purchase prices creep up.

↓

Throughput margin falls from 21.4% to 16.4%.

Not one of these steps was invisible. The chain was.

This is the slide of Day 1. Read it slowly, one line at a time. Do not paraphrase — let each link land before the next.

The teaching point: every single link was measurable and somebody owned it. Nobody owned the chain. That is why it survived eight months.
