IV · Pair exercise · 10 minutes

Your customer. Your constraint. Your agent.

Systems · Constraints · Agents

In pairs

•Pick a real business process from a customer you know — yours or your partner's

•Name the likely constraint, with one symptom that supports your guess

•Is it agent-shaped? Why, or why not?

•If yes — sketch the five subsystems for the agent in one line each

•If no — what should be done first?

Three pairs will share. 90 seconds each.

Most pairs will rush to 'design the agent' and skip the constraint diagnosis. Walk the room and gently redirect — the value is in steps 1–3 of the worksheet, not step 4.
