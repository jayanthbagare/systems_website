I · Behaviour

Structure generates behaviour.

Systems · Constraints · Agents

Four shapes account for most of what a system does over time. Each one comes from an arrangement of loops and delays — not from the people inside it.

Exponential growth

A reinforcing loop running unopposed. Nothing is pushing back — yet.

Goal-seeking

A balancing loop closing a gap. Fast at first, then slower as the gap shrinks.

S-shaped

Reinforcing first, then a balancing loop takes over. Growth into a limit that holds still.

Oscillation

A balancing loop with a delay. It overcorrects, every single time.

Ask what shape your metric has been making for the last six months. The shape names the loop structure before you have drawn a single arrow.

The point to land: behaviour over time is evidence about structure. If a team is oscillating, stop asking who is inconsistent and start looking for a delay in a balancing loop. The oscillation is the delay's signature.
