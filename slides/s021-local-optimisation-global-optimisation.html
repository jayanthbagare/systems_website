I · The trap

Local optimisation ≠ global optimisation

Systems · Constraints · Agents

Every team doing its best at its own metric will not, in general, produce a system doing its best at the metric that matters.

Dev team

Ships 30% more story points

Backlog of unreviewed code grows

Maintenance

Closes more work orders weekly

Closes the easy ones; criticals age

Procurement

Negotiates 8% lower spare cost

Lead times rise; technicians wait

The local-global gap is everywhere in our customer's operations and in our own. If you take only one thing from Block I, it is suspicion of any improvement measured locally.
