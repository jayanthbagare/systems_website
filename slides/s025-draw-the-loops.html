Systems · Constraints · Agents

Meridian's procurement system on one sheet of paper. No data yet — just structure.

What you do

Name the stocks — the things that accumulate.

Draw the flows between them.

Mark every loop R (reinforcing) or B (balancing).

Mark every delay with a double bar.

What you produce

One causal loop diagram on a flipchart.

At least one reinforcing and one balancing loop.

One circled thing you would need to measure and cannot.

How you know you're done

You can point at any loop and say what happens to the whole system if you speed it up.

Constraint for this lab: no laptops. Paper only. The tool is the thinking, and a screen lets one person do the thinking for five.

PM draws · EM keeps time · developers argue about the arrows

Enforce the no-laptops rule. It is the single highest-leverage constraint in the whole workshop — it forces the whole pod into one conversation.

Pods will want to draw a left-to-right pipeline. Push them: where does an arrow come back? If nothing returns, they have drawn a process, not a system.

At 40 minutes, tell every pod to stop drawing and start labelling R and B. They will not do it unprompted and it is where the learning is.
