One movement, two days

See → measure → choose → build → turn the lens on yourselves.

Two peaks, two silences. Day 1 peaks on someone else's system — the chain nobody owned. Day 2 peaks on their own — the pod naming its own constraint. Each day ends quiet. Do not fill the silence.

If you remember one thing about the arc: the two peaks rhyme. On Day 1 the room discovers that every link in Meridian's chain was measured and owned, and the chain itself was not. On Day 2 they find the same thing between the boxes of their own organisation — the seam — and then in their own delivery cycle.

The dip at the start of Day 2 is deliberate. Seeing the Shape is relief: most problems settle at automation, and that is clarity, not failure. Do not rush past it to get to the harness.
