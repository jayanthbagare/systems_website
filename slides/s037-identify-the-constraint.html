Identify the constraint.

Where does work pile up? Where do downstream stations idle waiting? What does your gut say versus what does the queue data say?

Symptoms to look for

•Work-in-progress visibly accumulates in one place

•One team is always “behind”; others are politely waiting

•Expediting at that stage produces immediate improvement

•Investing elsewhere produces no improvement at all
