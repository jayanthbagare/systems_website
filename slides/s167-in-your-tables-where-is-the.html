In your tables — where is the constraint?

Systems · Constraints · Agents

The setup

The flow from earlier: Sales Order -> Work Order & Planner -> Vendor Selector -> RFQ & Bids -> Shortlist.

Discuss in your tables

•Where do you think the constraint is, and why?

•What would you do to exploit it before elevating it?

•What measurement would prove (or disprove) your guess?

Walk the room. Listen for: people identifying the planner, people identifying the crew, people identifying parts/permits. All are defensible first guesses. The reveal will be more interesting if the room is divided.

Cap at 10 minutes. Two or three brief share-outs.
