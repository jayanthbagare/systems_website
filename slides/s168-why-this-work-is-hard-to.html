II · Anatomy of the constraint

Why this work is hard to scale

Systems · Constraints · Agents

Context-heavy

Asset history, recent failures, P&IDs, recent operator notes, supplier lead times — all loaded into the planner's head, per notification.

Judgement-shaped

Pool viability, risk weighting, terms to offer — none of this is a lookup. It is decision under uncertainty.

Coupled to the rest

Plan fit means cross-checking budget, deadlines, category capacity and competitor demand. Many ledgers, one decision.

Hard to outsource

Specialist knowledge of these vendors, these categories, these relationships. Hiring scales linearly; learning scales slowly.
