III · What does not change

What stays with the buyer

Systems · Constraints · Agents

The agent is not a replacement. It is a pre-processor that returns the planner's attention to where it actually matters.

•Safety-critical decisions

•Trade-offs that affect a customer commitment

•Anything that requires a named, accountable human

•Cross-category judgement — what should be deferred and why

Make this point firmly. The pitch is not 'fewer planners'. The pitch is 'the same planners, doing far more of what only they can do.' That framing wins both with the planners themselves and with safety/regulatory stakeholders.
