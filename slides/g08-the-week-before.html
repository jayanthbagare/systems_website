Presenter's guide · Preparation

The week before, and how to find your way around

Prepare

  • An API key for every pod. Labs 4 and 5 are dead without one.
  • The repo running on every laptop before the first lab — 23 CSVs in output_v2 #7. EMs own this.
  • Regenerated the dataset? Re-check the figures on #35 and #44 before you teach them.
  • Re-check the middle-tier vendors and numbers #59. They change month to month.
  • Paper: flipcharts and markers for Lab 1, index cards for the face-down answers, the one-page template #45.
  • Two facilitators: split the pods in half. Each of you picks the debrief pods from your half.

Find your way

  • P opens presenter view — current and next slide, notes, timer — on your laptop; the projector follows.
  • N shows notes on the main screen, for rehearsal only.
  • H includes hidden slides. They are kept for questions: the five subsystem drill-downs, the harness comparison table, the Jev and Laya reference, epistemology, system archetypes.
  • A number, then Enter jumps to that slide. Numbers in this guide count only the slides in the show.
  • Running order lives in slides/manifest.js. Hide, don't delete.

If you are presenting this deck for the first time, rehearse Day 1 Act II and Day 2 Act IV out loud with a timer. Those are the two places where pace decides whether the turn lands.
