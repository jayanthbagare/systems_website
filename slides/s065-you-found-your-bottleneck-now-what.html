III · A familiar moment

You found your bottleneck. Now what?

Systems · Constraints · Agents

The reflex answers usually arrive in this order:

→

Hire more planners

Linear. Helps. Hits a ceiling on availability and learning curve.

→

Outsource the work

Trades cost against context loss. Specialised plant knowledge does not transfer well.

→

Better tooling, dashboards

Helps if it removes friction — almost never enough alone.

→

Throw an AI agent at it

Sometimes brilliant. Sometimes a step-4 spend with no step-1 seeing.

The question is not whether to use an agent. It is whether the constraint is the kind an agent can dissolve.
