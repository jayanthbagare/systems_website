I · Systems thinking

A system is not the sum of its parts.

It is the pattern of relationships among them.

Disassemble a car into a heap of parts and you have the same matter — and no car. The car was the interactions, not the inventory.

Systems · Constraints · Agents

This is the foundational move. Once we accept that behaviour is in the relationships, we have to stop trying to improve a system by improving its individual parts in isolation. That is the trap of local optimisation — and it is the default behaviour of most organisations.
