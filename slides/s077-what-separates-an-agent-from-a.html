LAB 4 · The bar

What separates an agent from a threshold alert

Systems · Constraints · Agents

It investigates

Snapshot first, then it decides who deserves a deeper look. Fetching everything for everyone is not judgement, it is a cron job.

It attributes cause

Kovacs is declining because we paid late. Guangzhou because of duties. Same symptom, opposite action.

It stays quiet

A stable low scorer and a new vendor with thin data do not warrant an alert. Alert fatigue is a failure mode, not a tuning detail.

It recommends precisely

‘Improve the relationship’ is not an action. ‘Pay V001 on time for two cycles and give them first refusal on the next two Mechanical RFQs’ is.

Use this list twice — once to brief the lab, once to grade it. Pods should score their own agent against it before they present.

Show this at the start of Lab 4 and again at the debrief. Pods that self-score honestly learn more than pods you grade.

The 'it stays quiet' column is the one nobody anticipates. A pod whose agent alerts on twelve vendors has built a threshold, not an agent.

The Kovacs / Guangzhou distinction is the sharpest teaching moment in Day 2 — one is our fault and fixable, the other is structural and needs replacing.
