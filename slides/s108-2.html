Three anchors to carry in

Systems · Constraints · Agents

1

Find before you fix

Identify the constraint before designing an agent. Most teams will skip this step. Yours should not.

2

Is it agent-shaped?

Synthesis, classification, drafting under uncertainty — agent. Physical capacity, hard latency, regulatory gates — not (yet).

3

The harness, not the model

Most reliability comes from instructions, tools, environment, state, and feedback. Get the kitchen right before complaining about the chef.
