Seeing the System

Building at the Constraint

A two-day workshop for delivery pods · Systems thinking · Theory of Constraints · Throughput accounting · Agents

Jayanth, Bharath · SAP

Open quietly. Let the title sit for a beat before speaking.

Today is not about AI tools. It is about learning to see — to see where a system is actually constrained, to understand why most of our improvement efforts miss that place, and only then to ask whether an agent is the right answer there. We will end with a method you can apply directly in the hackathon and in customer conversations.
